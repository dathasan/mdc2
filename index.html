Treatments & Procedures

How We Can Help Your Smile

Complete, patient-friendly dental care for kids, adults, and seniors in Fatullah.

Dental Checkup

Thorough examination of your teeth and gums, pinpointing issues early before they become painful.

Scaling & Polishing

Gently removes stubborn tartar, plaque, and tea or tobacco stains to freshen breath and protect gums.

Tooth Filling

Tooth-colored, durable composite fillings that match your natural enamel and stop cavities from growing.

Root Canal Therapy

Save heavily infected teeth and relieve severe toothaches using pain-managed, modern root canal care.

Painless Extraction

Gentle, quick removal of broken, untreatable teeth or painful wisdom teeth with local numbing.

Crown & Bridge

Natural-looking ceramic and zirconia caps to reinforce cracked teeth or replace missing teeth permanently.

Custom Dentures

Complete and partial removable dentures tailored for seniors to enjoy easy eating and clear speech.

Smile Makeover

Closing tooth gaps, reshaping uneven edges, and aesthetic veneers to give you a confident smile.

Braces & Alignment

Modern orthodontic braces and aligners to fix crooked, crowded, or protruding teeth.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, friendly care designed to keep children calm and happy while treating milk teeth and cavities.

Gum Care

Effective treatment for bleeding gums, swelling, and loosening teeth to keep roots strong.

Urgent Dental Relief

Quick walk-in support for severe toothaches, cracked teeth from accidents, or sudden swelling.
